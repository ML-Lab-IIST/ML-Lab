Brain-Computer Interface Device

The lab uses the OpenBCI Cyton+Daisy, an advanced, open-source biosensing platform for EEG, EMG (electromyography) and ECG (electrocardiography) data acquisition. It provides 16 high-resolution channels, a 24-bit ADC, and wireless connectivity via Bluetooth or USB, and is compatible with OpenBCI GUI, Python, MATLAB and BrainFlow — making it well suited for BCI research, neurofeedback and brain-controlled applications.

Quick Setup Guide

  • Ensure the Cyton+Daisy board is fully charged before use.
  • Connect the OpenBCI USB dongle to your computer.
  • Power on the Cyton board using the switch on the side.
  • Pair the device with your computer via Bluetooth (RFduino) or USB.

  • Use the conductive gel for better signal quality.
  • Attach electrodes securely according to the required montage (EEG, EMG, ECG).
  • Ensure good skin contact and minimize movement artifacts.

  • Open OpenBCI GUI or BrainFlow-compatible software, downloadable from openbci.com/downloads.
  • Select the Cyton (16-channel) mode in the GUI.
  • Click "Start Data Stream" to begin recording signals.
  • Adjust gain settings (1x, 2x, 4x, etc.) for optimal signal clarity.
  • Save raw data in *.csv or *.txt format and post-process with Python or MATLAB.
  • Apply filters to remove noise (e.g., a notch filter for powerline interference).

  • Do not expose the device to moisture or extreme temperatures.
  • Turn off the board after use to conserve battery life.
  • Store the device and electrodes in a dry, safe place.

Usage Guidelines
  • Software and OS Restrictions: Do not install or modify system software without administrator permission.
  • Storage Usage: Store large datasets in designated directories to avoid system slowdowns.
  • Internet Usage: Do not download unauthorized software or visit restricted websites.
  • Data Security: Back up your work regularly to avoid data loss.
  • Power Management: Shut down systems properly after use.

For technical support or issues with any system, contact the course instructors.

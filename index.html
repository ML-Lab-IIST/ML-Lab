About Our Lab

The Machine Learning Lab is dedicated to advancing the field of artificial intelligence and machine learning through cutting-edge research and innovation. Our team of experts works on a variety of projects that aim to solve real-world problems using AI technologies.

Join us on our journey to explore the fascinating world of AI and make impactful contributions to the field.

What We Work On

Research Areas

Our work sits at the intersection of theory and application, combining mathematical rigor with practical machine learning systems.

Graph Neural Networks

Learning representations over graph-structured data for prediction and reasoning tasks.

Topological Data Analysis

Using topology to uncover the shape and structure hidden within complex, high-dimensional data.

Brain Computer Interface (BCI)

Decoding neural signals to build communication and control systems driven directly by the brain.

Kernel Theory

Developing kernel-based methods for learning in structured and non-Euclidean spaces.

Natural Language Processing

Building models that understand, generate and reason over human language.

Graph Federated Learning

Collaborative learning over graph-structured data distributed across decentralized clients while preserving privacy.
